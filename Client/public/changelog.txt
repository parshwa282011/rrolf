== November 12th 2026
[gardn] New mythic petal, the Iris: 25 health, same reload as the epic Iris, and it deals 10 poison damage over 18 seconds.
[gardn] New mythic petal, the Sponge: it has no collisions, so it can't be broken, and it soaks up unlimited damage and slowly deals it back over 20 seconds. If you take it off, the damage it still holds hits you at once.
[gardn] Both sponges (the legendary one and the new mythic one) show in their loadout slot how much damage they are holding.
[gardn] New legendary petal, the Dandelion: it stops healing on whatever it hits like the rare one, and you can shoot it at your enemies like the epic Missile.
[gardn] New legendary petal, the Fastest: a better Faster petal (8 health, 10 damage) that makes your other petals spin twice as fast as the Faster does.
[gardn] With an Antennae or an Observer equipped you can scroll to zoom between the normal view and the full view of the petal.
[gardn] In the Petal Gallery a new button lets you select petals to auto-delete: click the petals you don't want and, with Auto-delete drops on in the settings, they are deleted when you touch them while your loadout and inventory are full.
[gardn] Auto attack (K) and auto defend (L), like in Rysteria: they hold the button for you while you press neither. With both on you attack and defend at the same time, and the petals that only defend stay in close while the others go out. They are also in the settings.
[gardn] Hold ' to see what each dot color of the observer means (the mobs you selected) under the leaderboard.
[gardn] Every mob can spawn in the PVP area of the sandbox now (the worker, soldier and queen ants and the digger too), not only the ones of the first zone.
[gardn] Three clovers that bring luck to the biome you are in, and keep it while you have them equipped (broken or not): the legendary Mob Clover (3 leaves) makes rarer mobs spawn more often, the legendary Drop Clover (4 leaves) makes mobs drop their petals very slightly more often, and the mythic Clover (5 leaves) does both, with 0.75 times the luck of the two legendaries together. The luck of several clovers adds up, up to a limit.
[gardn] New Observer ability: in the Mob Gallery you can select mobs, and with an Observer equipped each selected mob shows as a dot of its own color on the minimap.
By: Parshwa

== November 8th 2026
[all] The stats pages show the icon of every petal: the loadout of a player in Rysteria, the petals that are saved in Gardn.
[gardn] The mobs in the sandbox's developer menu have their icons.
[gardn] The leader's curse (the mobs going for them, the damage at level 90) leaves a flower alone for its first 10 seconds after it spawns or is restored. There is no curse in the sandbox.
[rysteria] Snail run: the snail is epic 65%, legendary 25%, mythic 9%, exotic 0.75% or ancient 0.25% of the time.
[rysteria] The snail takes the cheapest way home: it goes round the hard parts of the garden, even when that is a longer walk.
[rysteria] The snail only wakes when a player is right next to it. Until then no mob can come within 500 units of it and none goes for it.
[rysteria] Ant holes are the size of a common ant hole at every rarity (a higher rarity hole only has more ants in it).
[rysteria] The snail's rewards (shell, mint, nazar) are drops on the ground at your feet, for everybody in the game, not only the players near the snail.
