Rysteria

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Rysteria is a browser game (and its Android and iOS apps, which only show the same website). It is run by one person as a hobby project at game.parshwa.blog. This policy says what information the game collects, why, and what you can do about it. "We" means the person who runs Rysteria; you can reach us at parshwashah282011@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account details. The username you choose, your email address (when you sign up with email), a random account id, and when the account was made and last used. Your password is never stored: we keep only a salted hash (argon2id) of it.
  • Sign in with Discord, Google or Apple. If you use one of these, the provider tells us your account id with them, your name or display name, and your email address (when you allow it). From Google we ask only for the basic openid, email and profile permissions; from Discord only identify and email; from Apple only your name and email. We do not receive your password with them, and we do not read your contacts, files, messages or anything else in those accounts.
  • Game data. Your progress: level and experience, petals and items, loadouts, nickname, kills, scores and saved runs.
  • Public data. Your username and scores appear on the public leaderboards, and anyone with your account id (or your username) can open your public stats page. Chat messages are seen by the other players in the same game and may also be written to the server's logs.
  • Technical data. Your IP address and browser details reach our servers (through Cloudflare) every time you connect. We use the address to deliver the game and to limit abuse (for example, too many login attempts); these limits are kept in memory for a short time. Server logs may contain it for troubleshooting.
  • On your device. The game stores your login (an account id and two sign-in tokens) and a cache of your game data in your browser's local storage so that you stay logged in. We do not use cookies, advertising or analytics trackers.

What we use it for

  • to run the game, log you in and keep your progress;
  • to send you one-time codes: confirming your email address, resetting a password, changing a username (by email, or as a Discord direct message if you pick that);
  • to show leaderboards and stats;
  • to keep the service secure and fair (rate limits, banning cheaters and abusive players).

We do not sell your information, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not send marketing email.

Information we get from Google

Rysteria's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use your Google email address and name only to create and log in to your Rysteria account, we do not share them except as described below, and we do not use them for advertising.

Who else handles it

  • Cloudflare carries the traffic between you and our servers.
  • Amazon Web Services (Amazon SES) delivers the emails we send: it receives your email address and the text of the message.
  • Discord, Google and Apple handle your sign in if you choose them (see their own privacy policies), and Discord delivers the direct message if you ask for a code there.

The game servers run on our own computers. We do not share your information with anyone else, except when the law requires it or to protect the service and its players from abuse.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and game data while your account exists. Backups of the database are kept for a limited time and are replaced as new ones are made. Inactive accounts may be removed.

Your choices

Deleting your account. You can delete your account yourself, in the game's account page (game.parshwa.blog/account, also reached from the Account screen in the game and in the apps): it asks for your password, a code we send to your email (or Discord) and your username typed back, so nobody else can do it for you. It removes your account, email address, linked sign-ins, progress, items, saved runs and leaderboard places at once. If you signed in with Apple we also tell Apple that your sign-in token is no longer needed. Copies in backups disappear when those backups are replaced (within 30 days). If you can not use the page, write to parshwashah282011@gmail.com and we delete it for you after checking that you own the account.

Other choices. You can ask us to show you what we hold about you or to correct it (same address). On the account page you can also unlink Discord, Google or Apple and add an email address, and you can clear the login from your browser at any time by logging out.

Security

Connections use HTTPS, passwords are hashed, and sign-in tokens are kept apart from the game servers. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a password you do not use anywhere else.

Children

Rysteria is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you think a child has made an account, write to us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date.
