Rysteria Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

This policy explains what information Rysteria (the game, in your browser or as an iOS or Android app) collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. It is published by {{DEVELOPER_NAME}} (“we”, “us”). You can reach us at {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}.

1. Information we collect

WhatDetails
Account information You can play as a guest. If you sign in with Discord, we receive your Discord user ID and username. We use the Discord “identify” permission for this, and the “guilds.join” permission, which lets us add you to our community Discord server. We never receive your Discord password.
Gameplay data Your display name, progress (such as level, experience and the petals you own), squad membership, PvP points and leaderboard standing, and your actions in the game.
Chat Messages you send in the in-game chat are shown to other players and may be logged to moderate abuse.
Technical data Your IP address when you connect to a game server, and basic device and browser information. Our servers use this to run the game, to stop abuse and duplicate connections, and to keep logs.
Data stored on your device The game saves an account identifier, a sign-in token and your settings in your browser or app storage, so you stay signed in and keep your settings.
Advertising data (mobile apps only) If ads are enabled, Google AdMob collects your device’s advertising identifier, IP address, device and app information and how you interact with ads. This is collected by Google, not by us. See section 4.

2. How we use information

  • To run the game: sign you in, save your progress, match you with other players and show leaderboards.
  • To keep the game safe: detect cheating, spam and abuse, and enforce our rules.
  • To fix problems and improve the game.
  • To show ads in the mobile apps, which helps pay for the game.
  • To answer you when you contact us and to meet legal obligations.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, our legal bases are: performing the service you asked for (account and gameplay), our legitimate interests in security and abuse prevention, legal obligations, and your consent for advertising identifiers and personalised ads.

3. Who we share it with

  • Discord, when you choose to sign in with it. Discord’s own privacy policy applies to that sign-in.
  • Google AdMob, in the mobile apps when ads are on (section 4).
  • Hosting and infrastructure providers that run our game servers and website, who process data for us under their own terms.
  • Authorities or other parties when the law requires it, or to protect the safety of players and our service.

Other players can see your display name, level, chat messages and leaderboard position. We do not sell your personal information for money.

4. Advertising

The mobile apps can show ads between screens (for example, after you press Continue on the death screen). Ads come from Google AdMob. Google and its partners may use your advertising identifier and other data to show ads and measure them, and, with your permission, to personalise them. Learn more at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: before any ads are requested we ask for your consent through Google’s consent form. You can accept, reject or change your choice at any time from the in-app settings.
  • iPhone and iPad: iOS may ask whether the app can track you across other companies’ apps and websites. If you say no, ads can still appear but will not be personalised using your advertising identifier. You can change this in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ads personalisation in your device’s Google settings (Settings › Privacy › Ads).

5. How long we keep it

We keep account and progress data for as long as your account exists. Technical logs are kept for a limited time and then deleted. You can ask us to delete your data at any time (section 6). Data held by Google, Discord or other providers is kept under their own policies.

6. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, or delete it, and you can object to or restrict some uses. To do any of this, email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}. We will answer within one month (or the period your local law sets). You can also:

  • Play as a guest, without signing in with Discord.
  • Clear the game’s browser or app storage to remove the data saved on your device.
  • Revoke the game’s access in your Discord settings (User Settings › Authorized Apps).
  • Change your advertising choices as described in section 4.

If you are in the EEA or UK you have the rights above under the GDPR and UK GDPR, and you may complain to your local data protection authority. If you live in California or another US state with a privacy law, you have the right to know what we collect, to delete it, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. We do not sell personal information. Advertising through AdMob may count as “sharing” for targeted advertising under some of these laws, and you can opt out as described in section 4.

7. International transfers

Our servers and the providers named above may be located outside your country, including in the United States. Where required, transfers rely on safeguards such as the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses.

8. Children

Rysteria is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

9. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information, but no online service can be completely secure.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. If the changes are significant we will tell you in the game or on our Discord server.
